Six policies, built to work together

Most businesses combine three or four of these. Your agent will tell you which ones your operations actually need and which ones you can skip.

General liability

Covers third-party bodily injury, property damage, and legal defense if a customer or visitor is hurt on your watch.

Professional liability

Protects against claims of negligence, missed deadlines, or errors in the advice or service you deliver.

Commercial property

Repairs or replaces your building, equipment, and inventory after fire, storm, theft, or vandalism.

Cyber liability

Covers breach notification, data recovery, and the legal costs that follow a ransomware or data-loss event.

Workers' compensation

Pays medical costs and lost wages for employees injured on the job, and satisfies state coverage law.

Commercial auto

Covers vehicles used for business  delivery, fieldwork, or transport  beyond what a personal policy allows.
