Every service tied to your policy, not just the policy itself

Coverage is the start of the relationship, not the whole of it. Here's what's actually included once you're a Meridian policyholder — the footprint, the claims process, and the support in between.

What's actually in each policy

General liability

Bodily injury & property damage
  • Defends you from customer slip-and-fall claims, property damage on client premises, and advertising injury up to your policy limit.
  • Defense costs outside the policy limit on most plans.
  • No extra surcharge on sub-contractor claims up to $250,000.
  • Claims hotline 24/7 with assigned named adjuster.

Professional liability

Errors, omissions & disputed work
  • Covers customer loss claims alleging negligence, errors in delivered plans, missed deadlines, or advice that caused a client financial loss.
  • Prior acts coverage for work completed before the policy started.
  • Contract dispute defense, including fee disputes that escalate.
  • Dedicated professional liability adjuster assigned for every file.

Commercial property

Building, equipment & inventory
  • Rebuilds or repairs your physical space and replaces what's inside it after a covered loss — fire, storm, theft, or vandalism.
  • Replacement cost valuation, not depreciated value, as standard.
  • Business income coverage for the period you can't operate.
  • Equipment breakdown add-on available at bind.

End-to-End Resolution Workflow

How a claim actually moves

The claim process is the same process for every loss — the only thing that changes is which specialist picks it up.

01

Report it any way that's fastest

File via your named agent directly, our 24-hour claims line, or the online portal. No form PDFs to print.

02

An adjuster is assigned

You get a name, a direct line, and a first assessment of what's covered before the day ends.

03

Investigation and documentation

Your adjuster gathers estimates, inspects damage, and flags anything that needs a specialist.

04

Fast-track on the undisputed amount

Where part of the claim is clear-cut, that portion pays out before the full claim closes.

05

Full resolution

96% of claims are fully resolved within ten days of being reported.

06

Prevention

Your adjuster reviews post-claim safety measures to protect renewal pricing.
